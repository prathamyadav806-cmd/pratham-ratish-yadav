MY LIFE AS A STUDENT

I'm Pratham Ratish Yadav, currently pursuing my second year of Mechanical Engineering at N.Y.S.S. Datta Meghe College of Engineering, Airoli , under the University of Mumbai.

I completed my schooling at P.V.G.'S Vidya Bhawan High School & Junior College , followed by my studies at Achievers Technical Academy .

Alongside my studies, I have one year of experience as an Educator at Gurukul Home Tuition , where I have developed communication, responsibility and practical problem-solving skills.

I started Pratuuu Editz as my own creative space, where I explore AI design, graphic design and video editing .
