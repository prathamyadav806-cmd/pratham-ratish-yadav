Smart Umbrella Stand

A concept-based solution designed to manage wet umbrellas indoors by drying them and collecting the water in a dedicated tray.

STATUS CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT
TYPE WORKING MODEL
ROLE PRESENTATION & MODEL SUPPORT
☂
01 — OVERVIEW

Solving a simple everyday problem.

A wet umbrella brought indoors can leave water on the floor and increase moisture around the entrance.

The Smart Umbrella Stand is proposed as a practical solution that combines umbrella storage, drying and water collection into one system.

02 — THE PROBLEM
01

Wet umbrellas create indoor mess.

When people return home during rainy weather, umbrellas are often placed near the entrance while still wet.

Water dripping from the umbrella can spread across the floor, create moisture and make the surrounding area difficult to keep clean and dry.

03 — PROPOSED SOLUTION
01

Umbrella Storage

Provides a dedicated place to keep a wet umbrella after entering the house or building.

02

Air Drying

A proposed air-flow mechanism helps remove moisture from the umbrella surface.

03

Water Collection

Water dripping from the umbrella is directed into a removable tray at the bottom.
